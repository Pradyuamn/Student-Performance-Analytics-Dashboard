# 🎓 Student Performance Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

This project presents an interactive **Student Performance Analytics Dashboard** built using Tableau.
The goal is to analyze student academic performance, identify at-risk students, and understand key factors influencing outcomes such as test preparation and subject-wise performance.

The dashboard transforms raw student data into **actionable insights** that can help educators and institutions improve learning outcomes.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🔢 Core KPIs

* **Average Score:** 62.20
* **Pass Rate:** 93.2%
* **At Risk Students:** 17.18%

These metrics provide a quick snapshot of overall academic performance.

---

### 📊 Student Segmentation

* Categorized students into:

  * High Performers
  * Average
  * At Risk

**Insight:**
Most students fall in the average category, while a significant portion (~17%) requires academic support.

---

### 📚 Subject Performance Analysis

* Compared performance across:

  * Math
  * Reading
  * Writing

**Insight:**
Math scores are slightly lower than reading and writing, indicating a need for improvement in quantitative skills.

---

### 🧠 Test Preparation Impact

* Analyzed performance based on test preparation completion

**Insight:**
Students who completed test preparation scored significantly higher, showing the strong impact of structured learning.

---

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters

* Gender
* Lunch Type
* Test Preparation Course

These filters allow dynamic exploration of the data.

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

* ~17% of students are at academic risk and may need targeted intervention
* Test preparation has a **positive impact on performance**
* Math is the most challenging subject compared to others
* Majority of students are clustered in the average performance range
